Budget 2026 adds $4 billion in new taxes while projecting a record $13.3 billion deficit. The PST expansion raises costs for housing, hurts public safety, and makes B.C. the least competitive province in Canada.

Why This Tax Must Be Scrapped

B.C. business leaders are calling on the Province to reverse the PST expansion. Here's why it matters.

B.C. cannot afford policies that raise input costs, discourage investment, and weaken our competitive position. We urge the government to scrap these PST changes. We cannot tax our way to prosperity by targeting the essential services that build our homes, protect our streets, and grow our economy.
